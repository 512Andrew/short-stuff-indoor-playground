## Party packages

| Package | Weekday | Weekend | Included children | Total guests | Additional child |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Short Stuff Celebration | $295 | $345 | 10 | 25 | $19.99 |
| Short Stuff Super Bash | $495 | $545 | 15 | 35 | $29.99 |
| Short Stuff VIP Party | $795 | $845 | 22 | 50 | Not specified |

All packages include two hours of private play, children's tables and chairs, setup and cleanup, complimentary ice and water cups, evites upon request, and free admission for accompanying parents and guardians.

### Celebration

Includes basic paper goods in a selected color. The customer provides all food, juice, decorations, and personal touches.

### Super Bash

Includes a choice of custom 10-inch cake, two dozen cupcakes, or two dozen cookies; a choice of 17.5-by-48-inch custom banner, balloon arch, or balloon columns; one juice or water per child; and basic paper goods in a selected color. The customer provides food, additional drinks, and added decorations.

### VIP Party

Includes a themed custom 10-inch cake or two dozen cupcakes, two dozen themed cookies, a 30-by-60-inch custom banner, balloon arch or columns, two large one-topping pizzas, juice boxes for all children, themed paper goods and goodie bags, and a gift for the birthday child.

## Open play

Open play is for children 10 months through 12 years, with a toddler area for ages three and under. Walk-ins are welcome during regular business hours; no reservation is required. Accompanying parents and guardians are free.

## Memberships

| Membership | Price | Additional children | Guest benefit | Party credit |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Monthly | $49.99/month | $20/month each | One guest for $9.99 once/month | — |
| Six-month | $269 prepaid | $100 each/six months | One free guest/month | $40 |
| Annual | $499 prepaid | $180 each/year | One free guest/month | $75 |

All memberships cover one child and include unlimited open play during regular hours, one complimentary pair of socks upon joining, 10% off additional socks, free accompanying adults, 10% off parties, and 10% off merchandise. Annual members receive 15% off merchandise.

Memberships are assigned to registered children and are non-transferable. Unlimited play is subject to capacity. Monthly plans auto-renew until canceled under the membership agreement; six-month and annual plans are prepaid. Party credits have no cash value and do not apply to add-ons. Special events, private events, food, beverages, and separately ticketed activities are excluded unless stated.
